Internet of Things (IoT) Applications

The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the network of interconnected devices that can communicate and share data. IoT has diverse applications across various industries. In healthcare, IoT devices monitor patient health, track medical equipment, and enable remote diagnostics. Smart homes leverage IoT for automation and energy efficiency, allowing users to control devices remotely.

Industrial IoT (IIoT) is transforming manufacturing by connecting machines and sensors to optimize processes and improve efficiency. In agriculture, IoT is used for precision farming, monitoring crop conditions, and managing irrigation systems. These applications showcase the versatility of IoT technology in enhancing daily lives and business operations.
